# Visitor Photo Wall - Manager Brief

## Executive Summary

Visitor Photo Wall is a **real-time photo sharing platform** that captures visitor photos and displays them on a large screen instantly. This proof-of-concept demonstrates a modern, engaging way to create interactive experiences at events, retail locations, conferences, or any public venue.

**In Simple Terms:** Visitors take selfies on a tablet/phone → Photos appear instantly on a big screen → Creates buzz and engagement

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional photo walls or galleries at events have several limitations:
- Photos take time to print or process
- No real-time engagement
- Difficult to manage and update
- Limited interactivity
- No digital capture for later use

## ✅ Solution

An automated, digital photo wall system that:
- Captures photos instantly using any device with a camera
- Displays photos in real-time on large screens
- Stores all photos for later retrieval
- Requires minimal setup and management
- Creates immediate engagement and social buzz
